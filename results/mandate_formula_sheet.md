## What the enforced rules leave

- Of random 20-security portfolios drawn from the securities no exclusion forbids, **0.19%** satisfy every enforced rule (95% interval 0.15% to 0.23%).
- That share is what a sampler pays for: classical rejection needs about 1/share draws per valid portfolio, a quantum estimator about 1/√share queries. Near 100% there is nothing to gain from rule tightness, only from precision.
- Rules the quantum oracle cannot hold: min_groups, risk_limit.
