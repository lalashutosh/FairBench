AURORA SUSTAINABLE EQUITY FUND - INVESTMENT POLICY
(Fictional example written for the FairBench demo. Not a real fund.)

1. Objective
The Fund aims to deliver long-term capital growth by investing in companies that manage environmental, social and governance risks well.

2. Portfolio construction
The Fund holds between 18 and 22 equally weighted positions selected from the investment universe. No single holding may exceed 7% of the portfolio, and no more than 15% of the portfolio may be invested in any one sector. The portfolio is spread across at least eight sectors.

3. Geography and size
The Fund invests only in companies domiciled in the Nordic region or Western Europe. No more than 25% of the portfolio may be invested in companies from a single country. Companies with a market capitalisation below EUR 1 billion are not eligible.

4. Exclusions
The Fund does not invest in companies involved in the production of tobacco or in controversial weapons. Companies that derive more than 5% of their revenue from thermal coal are excluded.

5. Best-in-class screen
Within each sector, the companies whose ESG score falls in the lowest 20% of that sector are not eligible for investment.

6. Portfolio-level targets
The average ESG score of the portfolio must be at least 5% higher than the average of the investment universe. The portfolio's average carbon intensity must be at least 25% below that of the investment universe. On average, women must make up at least 34% of the board members of portfolio companies.

7. Climate commitments
At least half of the portfolio is invested in companies with validated science-based emissions reduction targets. The Fund aims to reduce the carbon intensity of the portfolio by 7% each year.

8. Risk
The expected tracking error of the portfolio against the investment universe will not exceed 6% per year.

9. Sustainable investments
At least 80% of the Fund's assets will be invested in companies whose activities are aligned with the EU Taxonomy.

10. Stewardship
The manager votes at all shareholder meetings and engages with investee companies on their climate transition plans.
